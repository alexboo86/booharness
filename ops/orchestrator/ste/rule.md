## `ASD-STE100`

Write each brief, report and message in `ASD-STE100` (`Simplified Technical English`).

- Use approved `words` only. Use each `word` with its one approved `meaning`.
- An instruction has a maximum of 20 `words`. A `description` has a maximum of 25 `words`.
- Write one instruction in each `sentence`.

- Use the active voice. Use `simple tenses`.
- Keep the `articles`. Put a maximum of 3 `nouns` together.
- A `paragraph` has one `topic` and a maximum of 6 `sentences`.

- Do not change `technical names`: code, commands, paths, `URLs`, `ids` and `quoted text`.
- Do not change the keys and the `evidence` of a JSON report.
- The `words` of this `project` are `technical names` too: `seat`, `lead`, `brief`, `digest`, `worktree`, `commit`, `review` and others.

These `words` are not approved. Use the `words` that follow them:
