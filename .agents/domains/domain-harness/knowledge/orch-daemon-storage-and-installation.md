## Orch daemon, storage and installation

`ops/orchestrator/` is the local Node/TypeScript daemon, using Node's type stripping. `ops/orchestrator/tsconfig.json` covers src; `check:harness-audit` typechecks it, unlike root typecheck. `ops/orchestrator/package.json` pins its runtime MCP SDK, imported only by `ops/orchestrator/src/http.ts`; restart does not install dependencies. Transport limits are 4 MiB per body and 100 JSON-RPC batch messages.

`ops/orchestrator/src/config.ts` selects per-user state; `ops/orchestrator/src/store.ts` migrates SQLite with WAL/synchronous writes and stores seats, turns, reports/grades and append-only events. Hot `SeatQuery` reads use role/title or role/author indexes rather than filtering all seats. The author index's json_extract expression must match the query byte-for-byte; queries include closed rows and order by created_at/id.

`main.ts restart --when-idle` persists a drain flag. New turns queue with pending start args, except scouts needed by waiting parents. Drain status exposes held turns and warnings. Idle exit lets launchd restart; queued turns resume before the flag clears, keeping crash recovery armed. Cancel clears the flag and starts only drain-held turns in seat sequence, refusing absent drains or begun exits. Any stop interrupts running turns only, preserving queued turns; recovery neither closes nor removes worktrees of seats with kept queues (`ops/orchestrator/src/seats-recover.ts`).

`ops/orchestrator/src/lock.ts` protects the singleton. `ops/orchestrator/src/http.ts` rejects non-loopback Host/Origin before routing MCP/dashboard; orch_status is live. `ops/orchestrator/install.mjs` generates LaunchAgent/Windows logon deployment, wired by `ops/bootstrap/install-harness.mjs` and `ops/bootstrap/mcp-servers.json`. Health exposes active turns and the fingerprint of src, lockfile and external static-import closure (`ops/orchestrator/code-hash.mjs`). Restart logic distinguishes idle/busy stale code; interrupted turns are not silently done (`ops/orchestrator/src/seats-digest.ts`).

`ops/orchestrator/src/chat.ts` stores chat_bindings, chat_posts and per-handle chat_receipts, indexed for last delivered/confirmed receipt lookup. A live join binds one session to one handle; operator/all are reserved. Same joins are no-ops; successors inherit receipts and supersede predecessors. Board release under the post guard transactionally ends a binding and adds an operator tombstone generation. Old sessions cannot reclaim across a newer live lead above their own/tombstone floor. Released polls stop and posts require rejoin. `ops/bootstrap/session-start.mjs` prints session/join information without auto-joining.

Chat MCP tools are declared in `ops/orchestrator/src/http.ts`: join, post and read. Post/read require matching live bindings; bodies hold 1–600 Unicode code points. Addresses target handles or all; omitted address is an uninjected log. Eligibility begins at first join and excludes own posts. Unread reads atomically deliver/confirm returned posts oldest first; all-history reads have no receipts (default 50, max 200). Delivery is at least once, not proof of action: offers write nothing, delivery follows output flush, confirmation requires delivery, and hooks stop at confirmation or two delivery attempts. Lost output/responses can duplicate delivery.

Held wake polls release for never-delivered addressed wake posts. A first reply to a lead whose wake reached the poster within 24 h gains wake automatically unless already answered. FYI skips promotion; FYI plus explicit wake or unknown class refuses before writing. Logs never wake. `ops/bootstrap/lead-hooks.mjs` wires lead-chat and lead-task routing to Claude-family/Codex leads, skipping seats. Task routing ignores short/no-memory-entry prompts and asks Jev per domain, printing page indexes above threshold once per session/domain.

`ops/bootstrap/lead-chat-hook.mjs` silently polls within a 300 ms default budget, at most once per 20 s/session. State expires after seven days; slot saves reload/merge only that session. Output caps post lines at 1500 UTF-8 bytes: first clipped at a code-point boundary, later non-fitting posts omitted whole. Printed ids get receipts; confirmation scans 512 KiB for exact transcript attachment/system markers. Hook context/watcher stderr sanitize controls and line breaks without altering stored/board text.

`ops/bootstrap/lead-chat-watch.mjs` long-polls with fixed watcher-start idle time, flushes output, records delivery and exits 2; it does not confirm. New watchers supersede old; mod polls outrank watchers. `ops/claude-mods/orch-board/hooks/wake.ts` holds a poll while idle, submits released batches and uses generations to drop replaced or mid-turn answers unreceipted. Socket/5xx failures retry every 5 s; other bad responses stop. Watcher deadline is 7100 s below the Claude-family Stop hook's 7200 s timeout. `ops/bootstrap/lead-hooks.mjs` wires asyncRewake only for Claude-family leads, not Codex; `scripts/lib/check-lead-chat-wiring.mjs` checks it. Sessions must start after installation; expired watchers re-arm on a later turn.

`ops/orchestrator/src/dashboard.ts` persists section folds in localStorage. Its chat panel lists live handles, held watchers and the newest 50 posts oldest first. Receipt/poll state distinguishes read, delivered, pending and no watcher; recent posts count pending without a poll, and non-wake posts wait for a turn. The operator composer has explicit Wake and empty To means all, unlike omitted tool To. Chat routes are loopback-only; posts require exact daemon Origin and X-Orch-Chat, receipts require the header with absent/exact Origin. There is no cross-origin preflight grant or authentication of local same-user processes. LAN routes return 404 and hide the panel.
