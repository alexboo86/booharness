## Lesson capture and delivery

`scripts/memory/drain-inbox.mjs` sends inbox lessons through the capture gate (dry by default, `--apply` writes), re-domaining only to an explicit route. The `**` catchall is valid for a lesson already filed there, never a silent destination from another domain. Gate outages are errors, not refusals.

memoryd's server-side capture guard is not authentication (`scripts/memory/store.mjs`): new lessons need the `links.gate {v, at}` stamp from `capture-gate.mjs` and the anchor's routed domain. Moving an anchor requires a newer stamp; changing a domain requires its route. Body-only, status-only and legacy rows are unchecked. `scripts/memory/reanchor-lesson.mjs --refile` captures into one explicit target domain, then retires the source into the returned id. Multiple domains or only `**` refuse; cross-domain moves without `--refile` refuse. Dry runs write nothing; failures stop the batch and distinguish applied, failed and untouched rows, with both ids after a failed retirement.

`ops/orchestrator/src/store.ts` persists full/indexed first-brief delivery in `lesson_deliveries`; older orch refuses a v4 database. Reports require one `lessonsApplied` answer per full lesson, repaired through `ops/orchestrator/src/report.ts`. Lead delivery is posted fail-open by `ops/bootstrap/domain-lessons-hook.mjs` to loopback `/lead-lessons` (500 ms; skipped in seats). `lead_lesson_deliveries` stores one row per session/lesson, upgrading indexed to full but never downgrading. `ops/orchestrator/src/lesson-stats.ts` counts these as role `lead`.

At worker turn completion, `ops/orchestrator/src/memory-proposals.ts` auto-applies lesson appends through `ops/orchestrator/src/lesson-gate-child.mjs`, isolating the synchronous git walk from the daemon. Other proposals await `memory_apply`. The digest and `wait --json` expose each ledger outcome; read-only seats are not auto-applied. A first coder/reviewer/bughunter brief without `files` queues with a warning because it receives no page or lessons.

A missing-anchor-only refusal is deferred when the anchor is a regular file in the seat worktree, or a remote reviewer's stored base/snapshot tree. `sweepDeferredLessons` runs at landing, recovery and throttled turn end (10 s), refuses rows older than 7 days and applies once the file enters a ref. A per-row guard prevents duplicate writes across sweep, recovery and explicit apply. Refusal classification strips Jev log envelopes; content refusals are final, service outages retryable. A brevity-only refusal gets one repair turn, never a second. The `memory_proposal_applications` ledger reserves `gate` before gating and `running` before append; recovery replays missing, `gate` and `retryable`, never `running` or `uncertain`, whose CLI may have written. Brevity is deterministic, without Jev (`scripts/memory/lesson-quality.mjs`).

`lesson_repeats` records a proposal duplicating a lesson or title/main lineage delivered on an earlier seat turn, or a reviewer's duplicate delivered in full to its author by review end; indexed delivery does not count. `recordLessonRepeat`/`confirmLessonDuplicate` in `ops/orchestrator/src/memory-proposals.ts` support local `lesson-repeat`: the first applied proposal with matching provenance is checked, self-duplicates refuse, absent provenance is a no-op, and `lesson_repeat_tasks` permits exactly one task per repeated lesson. `lesson-stats` reads orch.sqlite read-only and active lessons from memoryd without local fallback: deliveries, application answers, capture outcomes and repeats; missing answers are `notReported`, and dead weight requires three explicit not-applicable answers.

`ops/bootstrap/lesson-router.mjs:renderLessonContext` deduplicates path-scoped lessons and budgets whole bodies plus an index, never clipped rules. Anchored rules precede Jev candidates; scores below 0.6 leave file/glob/numeric anchors indexed, while a symbol named in the brief bypasses Jev. `ops/bootstrap/domain-lessons-hook.mjs` carries identical lesson/domain-skill content across deny runtimes. For leads it also delivers a domain page once per session/domain, routing sections deterministically and indexing the rest. Lessons and skill hints take budget priority; a shed page retries next edit. Compaction clears its `knowledge/<domain>` mark. On deny runtimes a page accompanies an existing lesson deny, never denies alone; seats get pages from orch context.

Lead Claude Read delivery shares that page mark but neither queries lessons nor denies, and emits nothing for a Read that code-intel-order refuses (`ops/bootstrap/code-intel-order-lib.mjs`). The installer wires Read with edit tools for Claude-family lead homes, not glm workers or codex/grok/kimi. Fast exits use `scripts/lib/domain-of.mjs` and the nearest valid `.git` directory or realpath'd gitdir target, otherwise `git rev-parse`.

Read-only scout/researcher/debater contexts omit lesson bodies (`ops/orchestrator/src/context.ts`). `ops/bootstrap/jev-gate.mjs:extractTask` refuses missing/ambiguous brief boundaries. `ops/bootstrap/jev-client.mjs` reads local/environment secrets and pins `JEV_MODEL` to jev-1.13.0, never jev-latest; answers, audit lines and ledger rows identify the model. `scripts/jev-regression/run.mjs` (not in this kit) replays labelled scope/router/grading cases; wrong-side non-borderline answers, drift over 0.15 or another model fail. Persisted verdicts retain the answering or cached model, null without a Jev decision; mixed-model batches retain per-item models and a null batch model.
