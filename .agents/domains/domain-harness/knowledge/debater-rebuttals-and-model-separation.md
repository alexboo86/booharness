## Debater rebuttals and model separation

`ROLE_POLICY.debater` has `turns: 2`; `roleTurnLimit` defaults absent turns to one (`ops/orchestrator/src/seats-defs.ts`). A sealed seat at its limit cannot be prompted; settling closes it only after nonqueued turns reach that limit, leaving a first-answer debater ready (`ops/orchestrator/src/seats.ts`). Scout, researcher and critic retain one question.

Turn two requires `rebut`, a done first turn of another debater with a parsed report. `rebuttalOf` (`ops/orchestrator/src/seats-prompt.ts`) validates before storage and appends claims/evidence/forLead verbatim to the stored prompt outside its 300-word count. Opponent text remains on the turn row: resumed turns skip `seatContext`, and opponents may close or be pruned while queued.

Same-title, same-repo open debaters are siblings (`debaterSiblings`, sharing `taskSiblings` filtering with reviewers, `ops/orchestrator/src/seats-scope.ts`). `refreshAvoid` (`ops/orchestrator/src/seats-create.ts`) compares resolved `seatModel` (explicit or matrix) to sibling models: automatic picks avoid matches; explicit matches refuse. `rebuttalOf` also refuses same-model rebuttals across titles. Reviewer/critic picks compare author/coauthor models as well as families; reviewers also avoid earlier task reviewers. Critics collect no earlier reviewer; debater models constrain debaters only. An emptied pool refuses with the rule instead of calling `pickFamily`. Coverage: `scripts/test-orch-seat-pick-model.mjs` (not in this kit).

`armRebuttalClose` (`ops/orchestrator/src/seats-close.ts`) starts an unref'd 60-minute timer after a ready first answer, closing only if still open without turn two. Recovery (`ops/orchestrator/src/seats-recover.ts`) re-arms the remaining interval from the first turn's end or closes immediately if expired; two settled turns close normally. It does not use the whole-day idle sweep. Role notes: `ROLE_NOTES.debater` (`ops/orchestrator/src/context.ts`); coverage: `scripts/test-orch-debater-rebuttal.mjs` (not in this kit).

Round-table debaters share `options`: 2–4 strings of at most 60 characters, no trimmed duplicates, refused for other roles. Open siblings must have identical lists; bare forks cannot share their title. Stored/in-flight request retries with different options refuse. Creation copies an open option-bearing sibling's `data.tableId`, else mints a cohort id (`tableMembers`, `ops/orchestrator/src/seats-scope.ts`). `leadHandle` stores the live handle bound to the supplied session, else null (`chatLiveHandle`, `ops/orchestrator/src/chat.ts`; `ops/orchestrator/src/http.ts`).

A table member's second turn requires `rebut: "table"`, collecting other members' done first turns as anonymous `Member A/B/...` labels by creation order (`memberLabel`, `ops/orchestrator/src/table.ts`). Bare debaters can rebut a member's turn id, parsing its table report contract. Both member turns require `vote {option, confidence 0..1, changedBy}` through `parseReport`, `reportContract`, `repairPrompt` (`ops/orchestrator/src/report.ts`) and seat-derived `tableContractOf`; bare debaters still reject vote as unknown. Only done turns vote. `tallyTable` uses turn-two votes: strict majority with at least two cast wins, otherwise `chairman`.

When no member can still produce turn two, one reserved-author `table` chat post wakes every distinct non-null lead handle, or logs without handles. `requireLive` rejects reserved handles, including previously bound ones. Post text is at most 600 code points: decision (title capped at 80), tally, fitting nonvoter labels and whole member lines with `+N more` overflow. Provisioning failure also triggers posting (`postTableAfterFailure`, `ops/orchestrator/src/seats-provision.ts`). `maybePostTable` (`ops/orchestrator/src/seats.ts`) and `chatPostTable` set the first member's `data.tablePosted` in the insert transaction, preventing duplicate settle posts. Coverage: `scripts/test-orch-table.mjs` (not in this kit).
