## Pick statistics and reviewer yield

`ops/orchestrator/src/pick-stats.ts` implements read-only pick-stats over `ops/orchestrator/src/store.ts:openReadOnlyStore`: turn outcomes, duration, tokens and grade by family/role/domain, with missing metrics n/a. Domains come from coder taskFiles; reviewers inherit their author's domains. Multi-domain cells overlap; the footer is distinct. Profile cells count once per family/role/recorded model/effort, retaining unrecorded n/a rows.

Review outcomes distinguish coder seats/corrections/reproduced blockers from reviewer verdicts. Shared `reviewerReviews` selects first valid reports: recall uses reproduced blockers found or missed among paired reviews of the same coder/diff with no intervening coder turn. Precision counts reproduced blocker/major findings, treating support-round withdrawal/downgrade as not reproduced; fewer than five findings is thin. `reviewerYieldCounts` groups family/model/effort/domain and sums known input/output/cache tokens while counting unknown usage; overlapping domains cannot be summed. Family picking does not consume these reports; legacy recommendation/shadow/pool rows remain optional readable data, not computed outputs.

`ops/orchestrator/src/retained.ts` links landings through Author/Authors trailers or clean `seat_land` records, preferring trailers, and measures surviving coder diff. `ops/orchestrator/src/land.ts` writes refs/orch/landed/<seat> before applying: exact landed patch on seat base through a temporary index, overwritten on re-land, warn-only failure and not pruned. Without it retention uses branch/closed tip; missing/base-equal tips are not committed, missing landing not yet landed.

`scripts/rtk-miss-mining.mjs` reads local seat transcripts/store without remote sessions and ranks normalized program shapes using orch's rewritten/missed/piped/no-filter verdict. It splits chains outside quotes, counts command substitutions separately and unwraps bash payloads; whole-line-only matches stay chains. Examples show missed parts with full-line context. wholeLineBlockers classifies constructs preventing rewriting. The report requires rtk on PATH.

`scripts/review-yield-by-class.mjs` reports reproduced blockers per review and median/total known tokens by changed-path class. Rows distinguish first review, same-seat recheck and router replacement; rechecks distinguish repeated/new blockers by same-file nearby-line or title overlap against earlier author reviews. First-review repeated counts are zero. Invariant-list brief groups share recall/precision totals, not yield totals.

`ops/orchestrator/src/seats.ts` owns seat create/prompt/wait/report/close. SeatManager retains fields and delegates bodies to sibling seats-* modules; moved bodies call through self members, retaining instance overrides (`ops/orchestrator/src/seats-create.ts`; `ops/orchestrator/src/seats-provision.ts`). Local read-only roles inspect lead checkout, remote read-only roles host checkout; editing/review roles use separate worktrees, with local mirrors for remote coders (`ops/orchestrator/src/seats-defs.ts`; `ops/orchestrator/src/worktrees.ts`). Snapshots include lead dirty work as synthetic commits without changing its index; reviewer base is the reviewed change commit.
